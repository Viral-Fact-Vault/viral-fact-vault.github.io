Data Deletion Instructions

Viral Fact Vault respects your right to have your data deleted. We store only the minimal data needed to publish content (operator OAuth tokens) and any message you send us directly — we do not build profiles on viewers.

How to request deletion

  1. Email viral.fact.vault@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include the platform and account/handle the request concerns so we can verify it.
  3. We will confirm and complete deletion of any data we control within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Platform-held data

For data held by a social platform (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Reddit, X), please also use that platform's own data-deletion tools, as we cannot delete data stored on their systems on your behalf.

Revoking access

If you authorized our application via OAuth, you can revoke it at any time from that platform's connected-apps / permissions settings; we delete the associated tokens when access is revoked.
